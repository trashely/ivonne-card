Questions, answered

Before we begin.

What types of homes do you design?

We work across HDB, BTO, condominium and landed homes, as well as selected commercial spaces.

Can you work with an existing style or furniture?

Absolutely. We can build around pieces you already love and create a cohesive palette around them.

Do you handle renovation works?

Yes. The studio can coordinate design development, documentation, renovation works and project scheduling as one connected process.

How do I get started?

Send us a short note about your property and what you have in mind. We'll arrange an initial conversation to understand the project.
